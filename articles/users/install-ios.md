## Before you start

You must use **Safari** on iPhone or iPad. The PWA install option is not available in Chrome, Firefox, or other browsers on iOS — this is an Apple restriction, not a TGD limitation.

## Installation steps

<ol class="art-steps">
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">1</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Open Safari and go to <span class="art-code">thegreekdirectory.org</span>.</strong>
      Wait for the page to fully load.
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">2</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Tap the Share button.</strong>
      On iPhone, it's the box-with-arrow-up icon (⬆) at the bottom center of the screen. On iPad, it's in the top toolbar.
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">3</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Scroll down in the share sheet and tap "Add to Home Screen."</strong>
      It has a plus-in-a-square icon. If you don't see it immediately, scroll down — it's below the row of app icons.
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">4</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Confirm the name and tap "Add"</strong> in the top-right corner of the screen.
      The name defaults to "Greek Directory" — you can change it if you like.
    </div>
  </li>
</ol>

The app icon now appears on your home screen. Tap it to launch the full-screen experience.

## What to expect after installing

- The app opens **full-screen** with no Safari address bar or tabs
- A **bottom dock** appears with navigation tabs
- On first launch, core pages and your starred listings are cached for offline use

## Troubleshooting

**"Add to Home Screen" is missing from the share sheet** — Make sure you're using Safari, not an in-app browser (like a browser opened from inside another app). Open Safari directly from your home screen.

**The icon looks like a generic bookmark** — This is a display issue that usually resolves after closing and reopening Safari, or restarting your device.
