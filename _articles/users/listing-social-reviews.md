## Social media links

If a business has connected social media accounts, their icons appear in the **Social Media** section of the listing page. Supported platforms include:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- YouTube
- TikTok
- LinkedIn
- X (Twitter)
- Up to 3 custom social profile links

Clicking any icon opens that platform in a new tab.

## Review links

The **Reviews** section shows links to the business's profiles on review platforms:

- Google Business Profile
- Yelp
- TripAdvisor
- Up to 3 custom review site links

Clicking a review link opens the business's review page on that platform, where you can read or leave a review.

## What if links are missing?

Social media and review links are optional. If a platform icon doesn't appear, the business hasn't added that link yet. If you know a link is missing or incorrect, use the **Suggest an Edit** button at the bottom of the listing page to let our team know.
