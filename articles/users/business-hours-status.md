## How hours are displayed

On a listing page, hours of operation are shown in a table for each day of the week — Monday through Sunday. Each row shows:

- The day name
- Opening and closing time in 12-hour format (e.g., 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM)
- **Closed** if the business doesn't operate that day
- **Open 24 Hours** if the business operates all day

Today's row is highlighted so it's easy to spot at a glance.

## The live Open/Closed badge

The top of the listing page shows a real-time status badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Open** | Currently within open hours |
| 🔴 **Closed** | Currently outside open hours |
| 🟡 **Opening Soon** | Opens within the next 60 minutes |
| 🟠 **Closing Soon** | Closes within the next 60 minutes |

The status updates automatically based on the current time and the business's saved hours.

## Timezone

Hours are evaluated in **Central Time (America/Chicago)** by default. Some listings may have a different timezone set by the owner, in which case the status reflects that timezone.

## No hours set

If a business hasn't entered their hours, no status badge appears and the hours section shows "Hours not available." This is common for newer listings or businesses with variable hours.
