## What each pin represents

Every pin on the map represents one business listing that has GPS coordinates saved. Listings without a valid address or coordinates don't appear on the map.

## Clicking a pin

Tapping or clicking a pin opens a **popup** with:

- **Cover photo or logo** (if available)
- **Badges** (Verified, Featured, etc.)
- **Business name** — tapping it opens the full listing page
- **Category**
- **Tagline**
- **Address and phone number**
- **Open/Closed status**

The popup also has an × close button in the top-right corner.

## Cluster pins

When many businesses are close together, they group into a **cluster** — a numbered circle showing the count of businesses inside. Tap a cluster to zoom in and expand it into individual pins.

## Navigating from a popup

From the popup, you can:

- Tap the **business name** to open the full listing page
- Tap the **phone number** to call directly (mobile)
- Tap **Get Directions** (if shown) to launch your map app
