## How to unstar a business

Unstarring works exactly like starring — it's a simple toggle:

1. Find the business on any listing card (on the listings page, search results, or starred view) or open its listing page directly
2. Tap the **gold star** (★) button
3. The star returns to its empty state (☆) and the business is removed from your favorites immediately

## Unstarring from the starred view

You can also unstar directly from your favorites list:

1. Go to your [Starred](/article/users/viewing-starred) listings
2. Tap the ★ on any card to remove it from your list
3. The card disappears from the starred view immediately

## Unstarring across tabs

When you unstar a business, all other open TGD tabs update immediately — the star icon changes back to ☆ in every tab simultaneously.

## Bulk removal

There's no "clear all favorites" button in the regular browser experience. To remove all starred businesses at once in the PWA app, go to **Settings → Reset App** — but note this also clears all other app data. Remove businesses one at a time if you want to be selective.
