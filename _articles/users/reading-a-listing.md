## Page layout overview

Each business has its own dedicated listing page at `thegreekdirectory.org/listing/[slug]`. The page is organized into sections from top to bottom:

### Photo carousel

At the top of the page is a photo carousel showing the business's uploaded images. If there are multiple photos, use the left/right arrows to scroll through them. Premium listings can show up to 15 photos; Free listings show 1.

### Business header

Directly below the photos, you'll see:

- **Business name** with verified checkmark (if applicable)
- **Badges** — VERIFIED, FEATURED, PREMIUM, or COMING SOON
- **Category and subcategory tags**
- **Tagline** — a short one-line description
- **Open/Closed status badge** — live status based on today's hours
- **Action buttons** — Call, Directions, Website, and any custom CTA buttons (FEATURED/PREMIUM)

### Description

A free-text description written by the business owner (or our team). May include formatting like headings and lists.

### Contact & location

- Full address with a link to get directions
- Phone number (tap to call on mobile)
- Email address
- Website URL
- Business hours for each day of the week

### Map

An embedded interactive map showing the business's pin. Tap the map to open it full-screen, or use the **Get Directions** button to launch your map app.

### Social media & review links

Links to the business's Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and any custom social profiles. Review links for Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor appear here too (if set).

### Additional info

Up to 5 custom label/value pairs set by the owner — things like Cuisine Type, Parking, Languages Spoken, Accessibility, etc.

### Owner info

If the owner has made their name and title visible, it appears here along with optional contact details.

### Related listings

At the bottom: other businesses in the same category nearby.

### Suggest an Edit

A link to submit a correction if any information on the page is wrong.
