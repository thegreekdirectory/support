## When to suggest an edit

Use the Suggest an Edit feature when you notice:

- A wrong phone number, address, or website
- Incorrect or missing business hours
- An outdated business name or description
- A permanently closed business still showing as active
- Missing photos, social links, or other information you know is correct

## How to suggest an edit

<ol class="art-steps">
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">1</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Open the listing page</strong> for the business with incorrect info.
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">2</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Scroll to the bottom</strong> and tap the <strong>Suggest an Edit</strong> button.
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="art-step">
    <span class="art-step-num">3</span>
    <div class="art-step-body">
      <strong>Fill in the form</strong> with the correct information and submit. Our team will review and apply the change.
    </div>
  </li>
</ol>

## What happens after you submit

Our team reviews all edit suggestions manually. If the information checks out, we'll update the listing. We may reach out if we need more details.

Edits are not applied instantly — it may take a few business days depending on volume.

## You don't need an account

Suggesting an edit is open to anyone — no login required. We just ask for a brief description of what needs to change and ideally a source (like the business's own website) that confirms the correct information.
