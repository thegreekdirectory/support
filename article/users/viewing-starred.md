## Three ways to see your starred businesses

### 1. The star icon in the header

Click or tap the **★ gold star icon** in the site header. This takes you to the listings page with the starred filter active — showing only your saved businesses.

### 2. The Starred page (`/starred`)

Visit `thegreekdirectory.org/starred` directly. This page lists all your saved businesses in the order you starred them (most recent first), with the same listing card layout as the main directory.

### 3. The PWA dock (installed app)

In the installed Greek Directory app, the bottom dock has a **★ Starred** tab. Tap it to go directly to your favorites. Starred listings in the app are available offline — you can browse them even without an internet connection.

## What the starred view shows

The starred view shows the same listing cards as the main directory. Cards show the business name, photo, category, location, and hours status — exactly as they appear in regular search results.

## If you see nothing

If your starred list is empty or your stars seem to have disappeared, see [Why Did My Stars Disappear?](/article/users/stars-disappeared).
