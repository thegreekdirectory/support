## Additional Info section

Below the main contact section, some listings include an **Additional Info** block with up to 5 custom label/value pairs. Business owners use this to share extra details relevant to their specific business. Examples:

- **Cuisine Type:** Greek, Mediterranean
- **Parking:** Free lot on premises
- **Languages Spoken:** English, Greek
- **Accessibility:** Wheelchair accessible entrance
- **Dress Code:** Smart casual

This section is completely custom — every business decides what to include.

## Custom CTA buttons

**FEATURED** listings can have 1 custom call-to-action button. **PREMIUM** listings can have 2. These appear prominently in the action bar at the top of the listing page alongside the standard Call, Directions, and Website buttons.

Examples of custom CTA buttons:
- **Book a Table** → links to a reservation system
- **Order Online** → links to a delivery platform
- **View Menu** → links to a PDF or menu page
- **Shop Now** → links to an online store

Tapping a custom button takes you directly to the URL the owner has set.

## What if there's no Additional Info?

Many listings don't use this section — it's entirely optional. Its absence doesn't indicate anything about the business.
