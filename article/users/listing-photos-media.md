## The photo carousel

At the top of every listing page is a **photo carousel**. If the business has multiple photos, left/right arrow buttons appear on the sides of the image. Tap or click the arrows to move through photos.

How many photos a listing can have depends on its tier:

| Tier | Max photos |
|---|---|
| FREE | 1 |
| VERIFIED | 1 |
| FEATURED | 5 |
| PREMIUM | 15 |

If a listing has no photos, a default image for the category is shown.

## Logo

Some listings show a small logo thumbnail overlaid on the photo carousel or in the business header. This is separate from the main photos.

## Video (Premium only)

**PREMIUM** listings can embed a video on their page. If a video is present, a video player appears in the media section below the photo carousel. Tap the play button to watch it. The video is embedded directly on the page — no need to open YouTube or another site.
