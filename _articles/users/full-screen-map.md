## Navigating to the full-screen map

Visit `thegreekdirectory.org/map` directly, or:

- On the listings page, toggle the map and use the full-screen expand button
- In the installed PWA app, tap the **Map** tab in the bottom dock

## What's on the map page

The full-screen map fills your entire browser window and includes:

**Category filter bar** — A row of category buttons at the top of the map. Click any category to show only pins for businesses in that category. Click it again to deselect.

**Location search** — A text field to jump to a specific city, state, or ZIP code. The map centers and zooms to that area.

**My Location button (📍)** — Centers the map on your current location (requires location permission from your browser).

**Reset view button** — Returns the map to its default view covering the whole US.

**All business pins** — Every listing with GPS coordinates shows as a pin. Clusters group nearby pins automatically.

## Filtering on the map page

The category filter on the map page is a quick toggle — it's separate from the listings page filter panel. You can select one category at a time on the map page.

For more advanced filtering (subcategory, hours, pricing, radius), use the listings page in map or split view mode instead.

## Using the map offline (PWA)

In the installed app, the map tiles are cached during your last online session. You can still pan and zoom the cached area offline, but live pin data (businesses) requires an internet connection to load.
