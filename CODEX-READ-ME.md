## Page layout overview

Each business has its own dedicated listing page at `thegreekdirectory.org/listing/[slug]`. The page is organized into sections from top to bottom:

### Photo carousel

At the top of the page is a photo carousel showing the business's uploaded images. If there are multiple photos, use the left/right arrows to scroll through them. Premium listings can show up to 15 photos; Free listings show 1.

### Business header

Directly below the photos, you'll see:

- **Business name** with verified checkmark (if applicable)
- **Badges** — VERIFIED, FEATURED, PREMIUM, or COMING SOON
- **Category and subcategory tags**
- **Tagline** — a short one-line description
- **Open/Closed status badge** — live status based on today's hours
- **Action buttons** — Call, Directions, Website, and any custom CTA buttons (FEATURED/PREMIUM)

### Description

A free-text description written by the business owner (or our team). May include formatting like headings and lists.

### Contact & location

- Full address with a link to get directions
- Phone number (tap to call on mobile)
- Email address
- Website URL
- Business hours for each day of the week

### Map

An embedded interactive map showing the business's pin. Tap the map to open it full-screen, or use the **Get Directions** button to launch your map app.

### Social media & review links

Links to the business's Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and any custom social profiles. Review links for Google, Yelp, and TripAdvisor appear here too (if set).

### Additional info

Up to 5 custom label/value pairs set by the owner — things like Cuisine Type, Parking, Languages Spoken, Accessibility, etc.

### Owner info

If the owner has made their name and title visible, it appears here along with optional contact details.

### Related listings

At the bottom: other businesses in the same category nearby.

### Suggest an Edit

A link to submit a correction if any information on the page is wrong.

## The three badges

### ✓ Verified

A **blue Verified badge** appears on listing cards and listing pages. It means The Greek Directory team has reviewed this business and confirmed it is legitimate and Greek-owned. The information has been checked for accuracy.

Verified listings also show a **blue checkmark** next to the business name anywhere it appears in the directory.

### ⭐ Featured

A **gold/amber Featured badge** means this listing receives **priority placement** in search results and appears in the Featured Businesses section on the homepage. Featured listings are more prominent throughout the directory.

### 💎 Premium

Premium listings have everything Featured listings do, plus additional features: up to 15 photos, an embedded video, and up to two custom call-to-action buttons on their listing page.

## What badges mean for you as a visitor

| Badge | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | The business has been reviewed and confirmed by TGD |
| **Featured** | The business is actively promoted in the directory |
| **Premium** | The business has the most complete profile available |
| **No badge** | A FREE listing — valid and searchable, just not additionally reviewed |

## How hours are displayed

On a listing page, hours of operation are shown in a table for each day of the week — Monday through Sunday. Each row shows:

- The day name
- Opening and closing time in 12-hour format (e.g., 11:00 AM – 9:00 PM)
- **Closed** if the business doesn't operate that day
- **Open 24 Hours** if the business operates all day

Today's row is highlighted so it's easy to spot at a glance.

## The live Open/Closed badge

The top of the listing page shows a real-time status badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Open** | Currently within open hours |
| 🔴 **Closed** | Currently outside open hours |
| 🟡 **Opening Soon** | Opens within the next 60 minutes |
| 🟠 **Closing Soon** | Closes within the next 60 minutes |

The status updates automatically based on the current time and the business's saved hours.

## Timezone

Hours are evaluated in **Central Time (America/Chicago)** by default. Some listings may have a different timezone set by the owner, in which case the status reflects that timezone.

## No hours set

If a business hasn't entered their hours, no status badge appears and the hours section shows "Hours not available." This is common for newer listings or businesses with variable hours.

## The pricing scale

Some listings display a pricing indicator — one to four dollar signs — set by the business owner. It's a general guide to the cost level relative to that type of business:

| Symbol | What it generally means |
|---|---|
| **$** | Budget-friendly / inexpensive |
| **$$** | Moderate / average for the category |
| **$$$** | Above average / upscale |
| **$$$$** | Premium / fine dining / luxury |

## It's relative to the category

Pricing is always relative to the business's industry. A **$$** restaurant and a **$$** law firm represent completely different actual price ranges. Think of it as "how does this business compare to others in its field," not an absolute dollar amount.

## Not all listings have it

Pricing is optional. Many listings — especially service businesses where costs vary by project — don't have a pricing indicator set. This is normal and doesn't indicate anything about the business's quality.

## The photo carousel

At the top of every listing page is a **photo carousel**. If the business has multiple photos, left/right arrow buttons appear on the sides of the image. Tap or click the arrows to move through photos.

How many photos a listing can have depends on its tier:

| Tier | Max photos |
|---|---|
| FREE | 1 |
| VERIFIED | 1 |
| FEATURED | 5 |
| PREMIUM | 15 |

If a listing has no photos, a default image for the category is shown.

## Logo

Some listings show a small logo thumbnail overlaid on the photo carousel or in the business header. This is separate from the main photos.

## Video (Premium only)

**PREMIUM** listings can embed a video on their page. If a video is present, a video player appears in the media section below the photo carousel. Tap the play button to watch it. The video is embedded directly on the page — no need to open YouTube or another site.

## Phone

If a phone number is listed, a **Call** button appears in the action bar at the top of the listing page (and in a sticky bar at the bottom on mobile). Tapping it opens your device's phone app with the number pre-dialed.

Phone numbers are stored and displayed in standard US format: (630) 555-1234.

## Email

If an email address is listed, it appears in the contact section. Tapping it opens your device's default email app with the address pre-filled in the To field.

## Website

If a website is listed, a **Website** button appears in the action bar. Tapping it opens the business's website in your browser. In the PWA app, external links open in your system browser instead of inside the app.

## Address

The full address appears in the contact section with a **Get Directions** button next to it. Tapping it launches your preferred map app with the address pre-entered as the destination. See [Getting Directions](/article/users/getting-directions) for more detail.

## What if contact info is missing?

Not all businesses have all contact fields. If a field isn't shown, the business either hasn't added it yet or has chosen not to display it.

If you know a business has a phone number or website that's missing from their listing, use the **Suggest an Edit** button at the bottom of the page to flag it.

## Additional Info section

Below the main contact section, some listings include an **Additional Info** block with up to 5 custom label/value pairs. Business owners use this to share extra details relevant to their specific business. Examples:

- **Cuisine Type:** Greek, Mediterranean
- **Parking:** Free lot on premises
- **Languages Spoken:** English, Greek
- **Accessibility:** Wheelchair accessible entrance
- **Dress Code:** Smart casual

This section is completely custom — every business decides what to include.

## Custom CTA buttons

**FEATURED** listings can have 1 custom call-to-action button. **PREMIUM** listings can have 2. These appear prominently in the action bar at the top of the listing page alongside the standard Call, Directions, and Website buttons.

Examples of custom CTA buttons:
- **Book a Table** → links to a reservation system
- **Order Online** → links to a delivery platform
- **View Menu** → links to a PDF or menu page
- **Shop Now** → links to an online store

Tapping a custom button takes you directly to the URL the owner has set.

## What if there's no Additional Info?

Many listings don't use this section — it's entirely optional. Its absence doesn't indicate anything about the business.

## What "Coming Soon" means

A **Coming Soon** badge on a listing means the business is real and Greek-owned, but is not yet fully open. The listing is published early so the community can discover and anticipate it.

Coming Soon listings are fully browsable — you can see the business name, category, location, description, and any contact info the owner has provided. Some fields may be incomplete until the business opens.

## Why listings go up early

Business owners sometimes claim or submit their listing before their doors open, so they can:

- Build awareness in the community ahead of opening
- Collect visibility and social media follows early
- Have their listing ready to go live the moment they open

## How to keep track of a Coming Soon business

**Star it** — Tap the ★ star button on the listing to save it to your favorites. You'll be able to find it easily in your [Starred](/article/users/starring-favorites) list once it opens and updates.

**Check back** — There's no automatic notification system for opening updates currently. Check the listing page again after some time to see if hours and full contact info have been added.

## Finding Coming Soon listings

Use the **Coming Soon** filter on the listings page to browse all businesses with this status. See [Other Filters Explained](/article/users/other-filters).

## What is a chain listing?

A **chain listing** is a location that is part of a multi-location Greek-owned business — for example, a restaurant group with three locations, or a bakery with two branches.

Each individual location has its own listing page with its own address, hours, phone number, and photos. They are separate listings that happen to share a parent brand.

## How chain listings are identified

Chain listings display the **chain or brand name** alongside the individual location name on their listing page. For example: "Athena Bakery — Lincoln Park" might be one listing, and "Athena Bakery — Wicker Park" another.

Both listings are discoverable through the same search — searching for "Athena Bakery" will show all matching locations.

## Finding all locations of a chain

The best way to find all locations of a chain business is to:

1. Search the brand name on the homepage or listings page
2. Use the **map view** to see all pins at once — chain locations in the same city will cluster together

There is no separate "chain overview page" currently — each location stands on its own.

## Three ways to use the map

| Mode | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Map panel** | Listings page (`/listings`) | Toggling a map above the grid while browsing |
| **Split view** | Listings page (`/listings`) | Side-by-side list and map simultaneously |
| **Full-screen map** | Map page (`/map`) | Visual exploration without the listings grid |

## Map panel (listings page)

Tap the **Map** button near the top of the listings page to toggle a map panel above the listings grid. The map shows pins for all currently filtered listings.

- **Zoom** — Use the `+` / `−` buttons or scroll wheel (desktop), or pinch to zoom (mobile)
- **Pan** — Click and drag, or swipe on mobile
- **Click a pin** — Opens a popup with the business name, category, and a link to its page
- **Close** — Tap the Map button again

## Full-screen map (`/map`)

The dedicated map page gives you the entire screen as a map. It includes:

- A **category filter** at the top to show only certain types of businesses
- A **location search** to jump to a specific city or ZIP code
- All the same pins and cluster behavior as the listings page map

## Map tiles and style

The map uses **OpenStreetMap** tiles. In dark mode (system or PWA setting), the map tiles are automatically inverted and re-colored to match the dark theme.

## What is split view?

Split view divides the listings page into two panels: the **listings list** on the left and an **interactive map** on the right — shown at the same time. It's the best way to browse geographically and compare businesses side by side.

## Activating split view

Click the **Split View** button on the listings page (next to the Map button). The page layout shifts to the two-panel view.

## How they interact

**Click a listing card** in the left panel → the map flies to that business's location and opens its popup automatically. The card is highlighted with a blue left border.

**Click a map pin** → the matching listing card in the left panel scrolls into view and is highlighted.

This two-way linking makes it easy to identify which pin belongs to which business without having to open individual pages.

## Navigating in split view

- The listing list on the left is scrollable independently of the map
- The map can be panned and zoomed freely
- All active filters still apply — the list and map both reflect your current filter state
- A **Visit** button appears on each listing card in the highlighted state, letting you open the full listing page without leaving split view

## On mobile

On screens narrower than 768px, split view stacks vertically — the list appears above and the map below. This lets you use both panels on mobile, just not side by side.

## Exiting split view

Click the **Split View** button again to return to the standard single-panel layout.

## What each pin represents

Every pin on the map represents one business listing that has GPS coordinates saved. Listings without a valid address or coordinates don't appear on the map.

## Clicking a pin

Tapping or clicking a pin opens a **popup** with:

- **Cover photo or logo** (if available)
- **Badges** (Verified, Featured, etc.)
- **Business name** — tapping it opens the full listing page
- **Category**
- **Tagline**
- **Address and phone number**
- **Open/Closed status**

The popup also has an × close button in the top-right corner.

## Cluster pins

When many businesses are close together, they group into a **cluster** — a numbered circle showing the count of businesses inside. Tap a cluster to zoom in and expand it into individual pins.

## Navigating from a popup

From the popup, you can:

- Tap the **business name** to open the full listing page
- Tap the **phone number** to call directly (mobile)
- Tap **Get Directions** (if shown) to launch your map app

## How marker layering works

When multiple business pins are close together on the map but not quite touching (not yet grouped into a cluster), they can overlap. The tier system determines which pin appears on top:

| Tier | Stack order |
|---|---|
| **PREMIUM** | Highest — always on top |
| **FEATURED** | Second |
| **VERIFIED** | Third |
| **FREE** | Lowest |

This means if a FREE listing and a PREMIUM listing pins overlap, the PREMIUM pin is visible and the FREE pin is underneath.

## Visual differences by tier

Pins also look slightly different by tier:

- **FREE / VERIFIED** — Standard blue-bordered circular pin
- **FEATURED / PREMIUM** — Gold-bordered pin with a subtle glow effect

The gold border makes Featured and Premium pins visually distinct even without overlapping.

## Hovering a pin

When you hover over any pin (on desktop) or tap it (mobile), it pops to the very top regardless of tier — so you can always access any business, even if it's behind a higher-tier pin. The hovered/tapped pin gets a temporary z-index boost to bring it forward.

## Why TGD uses your location

Location data helps TGD:

- Show nearby businesses higher in the default sort
- Center the map on your area automatically
- Enable the **radius filter** (distance-based filtering)
- Enable **Closest to Me** sort order

## IP-based estimation (no permission needed)

When you visit the listings page, TGD automatically estimates your location using your IP address. This requires no permission and happens invisibly. It gives a **city-level** estimate — accurate enough to bias nearby results and center the map on your city, but not precise enough for tight radius filtering.

You'll see an estimated-location circle on the map (a translucent ring) showing the approximate area.

## Precise location (browser permission)

For exact distance features, TGD requests your browser's GPS location. A permission prompt appears from your browser when you:

- Click the **📍 Locate Me** button on the map or listings page
- Use the radius filter for the first time

**Allow** → TGD gets your exact coordinates. The map centers precisely on you, the estimated-location circle is replaced with an accurate marker, and radius filtering becomes highly precise.

**Deny** → TGD falls back to the IP estimate. Radius filtering still works but is less accurate.

## Regranting permission after denying

If you denied location permission and want to re-enable it:

- **Chrome / Android:** Settings → Privacy and security → Site settings → Location → Find thegreekdirectory.org → Allow
- **Safari / iOS:** Settings app → Safari → Location → thegreekdirectory.org → Allow

## Privacy

Your location is only used on-device for filtering and sorting. It is not stored, sent to a server, or tracked.

## Estimated location (IP-based)

**What it is:** An automatic guess of your city based on your IP address, provided by [ipapi.co](https://ipapi.co). No permission required.

**Accuracy:** City-level — typically within 10–30 miles of your actual location. It may be off entirely if you're using a VPN or cellular data routed through a different city.

**What it enables:**
- Biases the default sort toward nearby listings
- Centers the map on your approximate city
- Shows a translucent estimated-location circle on the map
- Powers a rough version of the Closest to Me sort

**What it can't do reliably:**
- Accurate radius filtering under ~15 miles
- Precise Closest to Me sorting

## Precise location (GPS)

**What it is:** Your actual GPS coordinates from your device, requested via the browser's Geolocation API.

**Accuracy:** Within meters.

**What it enables:**
- Precise radius filtering (e.g., show businesses within 3 miles)
- Accurate Closest to Me sorting
- Exact location marker on the map (replaces the estimated circle)

**How to enable it:** Click the **📍 Locate Me** button on the listings page or map, then allow location permission in the browser prompt.

## Summary table

| Feature | Estimated (IP) | Precise (GPS) |
|---|---|---|
| Default sort bias | ✓ | ✓ |
| Map center | Approximate | Exact |
| Radius filter | Approximate | Accurate |
| Closest to Me sort | Approximate | Accurate |
| Permission required | No | Yes |

## How cross-tab sync works

TGD uses the browser's built-in `localStorage` storage event to keep star states synchronized across tabs. When you star or unstar a business in one tab:

1. The star state is written to `localStorage`
2. All other open TGD tabs receive the storage event automatically
3. Each tab updates the star icon for that business immediately — no refresh needed

This happens instantly and requires no server communication. It works as long as all tabs are in the same browser on the same device.

## What syncs and what doesn't

**Does sync across tabs:**
- Star / unstar actions
- The starred count in the header

**Does NOT sync:**
- Across different browsers (e.g., Chrome and Safari on the same computer)
- Across different devices
- Across different user profiles in the same browser

## Syncing across devices

There is currently no account-based sync for starred businesses. Stars are stored locally in your browser cookie and (in the PWA) in your device's IndexedDB. They exist only on the device where you starred them.

For the most seamless experience, install the PWA app on each device you use — your starred data stays available offline on each device independently.

Even in a regular browser (non-PWA), starring uses a `starredListings` cookie to track your saved IDs. This is lighter than IndexedDB — it stores only the list of IDs, not the full listing data or images. That's why offline viewing is only available in the installed app.

## Why stars disappear

Starred businesses are stored **locally in your browser** — in a cookie (`starredListings`) and, if you use the PWA, in IndexedDB. They are not stored in a server account tied to your identity. This means:

### Cause 1: You cleared your browser data

If you cleared cookies, browsing history, or "all site data" in your browser settings, your starred listings were deleted along with everything else. This is the most common cause.

### Cause 2: You're on a different browser

Stars saved in Chrome won't appear in Safari, and vice versa — even on the same device. Each browser has its own independent cookie and storage.

### Cause 3: You're on a different device

Stars don't sync across devices automatically. A business starred on your phone won't appear on your laptop.

### Cause 4: Cookie expiry or restriction

Some browsers are set to block or regularly clear third-party cookies and site data. If your browser privacy settings are aggressive, stars may be lost when you close the browser.

### Cause 5: You reset the PWA app

Using the **Reset App** option in the PWA Settings clears all local data including starred businesses.

## How to prevent losing stars in the future

**Install the PWA app** — IndexedDB data in installed apps is less likely to be cleared by routine browser data sweeps compared to regular browser cookies.

**Don't use "Clear all browsing data"** without understanding that it will remove your starred businesses.

**Star businesses across devices** — Since there's no sync, re-star important businesses on each device you use.
