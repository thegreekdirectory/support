## What is a chain listing?

A **chain listing** is a location that is part of a multi-location Greek-owned business — for example, a restaurant group with three locations, or a bakery with two branches.

Each individual location has its own listing page with its own address, hours, phone number, and photos. They are separate listings that happen to share a parent brand.

## How chain listings are identified

Chain listings display the **chain or brand name** alongside the individual location name on their listing page. For example: "Athena Bakery — Lincoln Park" might be one listing, and "Athena Bakery — Wicker Park" another.

Both listings are discoverable through the same search — searching for "Athena Bakery" will show all matching locations.

## Finding all locations of a chain

The best way to find all locations of a chain business is to:

1. Search the brand name on the homepage or listings page
2. Use the **map view** to see all pins at once — chain locations in the same city will cluster together

There is no separate "chain overview page" currently — each location stands on its own.
