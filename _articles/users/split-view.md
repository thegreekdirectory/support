## What is split view?

Split view divides the listings page into two panels: the **listings list** on the left and an **interactive map** on the right — shown at the same time. It's the best way to browse geographically and compare businesses side by side.

## Activating split view

Click the **Split View** button on the listings page (next to the Map button). The page layout shifts to the two-panel view.

## How they interact

**Click a listing card** in the left panel → the map flies to that business's location and opens its popup automatically. The card is highlighted with a blue left border.

**Click a map pin** → the matching listing card in the left panel scrolls into view and is highlighted.

This two-way linking makes it easy to identify which pin belongs to which business without having to open individual pages.

## Navigating in split view

- The listing list on the left is scrollable independently of the map
- The map can be panned and zoomed freely
- All active filters still apply — the list and map both reflect your current filter state
- A **Visit** button appears on each listing card in the highlighted state, letting you open the full listing page without leaving split view

## On mobile

On screens narrower than 768px, split view stacks vertically — the list appears above and the map below. This lets you use both panels on mobile, just not side by side.

## Exiting split view

Click the **Split View** button again to return to the standard single-panel layout.
