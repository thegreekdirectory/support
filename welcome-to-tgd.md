## What is The Greek Directory?

The Greek Directory (TGD) is America's premier online directory of Greek-owned businesses. Our mission is to connect the Greek-American community with the businesses that serve them — from family restaurants and Greek Orthodox churches to law firms, specialty markets, and professional services.

Whether you're looking for a Greek restaurant near you, need a Greek-speaking attorney, or want to support your local Greek community, TGD makes it easy to discover and connect with businesses that share your heritage.

## Who is it for?

TGD serves two main groups:

**Visitors & Community Members** — Anyone looking to discover, explore, or support Greek-owned businesses across the United States. You don't need an account to browse. Just visit the site, search, and explore.

**Business Owners** — Greek-owned business owners who want to be found by their community. Owners can claim their listing, manage their profile, upload photos, set hours, and track how customers engage with their page.

## Key features for visitors

- **Powerful search** — Find businesses by name, category, or location with real-time predictive results.
- **Advanced filters** — Filter by open hours, pricing, distance from you, subcategory, and more.
- **Interactive map** — See all listings on a full-screen map and switch to split-view mode.
- **Starred favorites** — Save businesses you love with the star button and access them any time.
- **Installable app** — Install TGD on your phone's home screen for a native app experience with offline support.
- **English & Greek** — Toggle between English and Greek (Ελληνικά) using the language switch in the header.

## Key features for business owners

- **Free listing** — Every Greek-owned business can have a basic listing at no cost.
- **Business portal** — A dedicated dashboard to edit your profile, upload media, set hours, and view analytics.
- **Analytics** — See how many people viewed your listing, called your number, visited your website, and more.
- **Tier upgrades** — Optional upgrades (VERIFIED, FEATURED, PREMIUM) unlock additional features like more photos, priority placement, and custom call-to-action buttons.
