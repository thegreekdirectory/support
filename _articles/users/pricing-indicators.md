## The pricing scale

Some listings display a pricing indicator — one to four dollar signs — set by the business owner. It's a general guide to the cost level relative to that type of business:

| Symbol | What it generally means |
|---|---|
| **$** | Budget-friendly / inexpensive |
| **$$** | Moderate / average for the category |
| **$$$** | Above average / upscale |
| **$$$$** | Premium / fine dining / luxury |

## It's relative to the category

Pricing is always relative to the business's industry. A **$$** restaurant and a **$$** law firm represent completely different actual price ranges. Think of it as "how does this business compare to others in its field," not an absolute dollar amount.

## Not all listings have it

Pricing is optional. Many listings — especially service businesses where costs vary by project — don't have a pricing indicator set. This is normal and doesn't indicate anything about the business's quality.
