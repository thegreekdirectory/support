## The three badges

### ✓ Verified

A **blue Verified badge** appears on listing cards and listing pages. It means The Greek Directory team has reviewed this business and confirmed it is legitimate and Greek-owned. The information has been checked for accuracy.

Verified listings also show a **blue checkmark** next to the business name anywhere it appears in the directory.

### ⭐ Featured

A **gold/amber Featured badge** means this listing receives **priority placement** in search results and appears in the Featured Businesses section on the homepage. Featured listings are more prominent throughout the directory.

### 💎 Premium

Premium listings have everything Featured listings do, plus additional features: up to 15 photos, an embedded video, and up to two custom call-to-action buttons on their listing page.

## What badges mean for you as a visitor

| Badge | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | The business has been reviewed and confirmed by TGD |
| **Featured** | The business is actively promoted in the directory |
| **Premium** | The business has the most complete profile available |
| **No badge** | A FREE listing — valid and searchable, just not additionally reviewed |

## The claimed checkmark

A green ✓ checkmark badge (separate from Verified) indicates the business owner has **claimed** their listing and actively manages it. This means the information is more likely to be current.
