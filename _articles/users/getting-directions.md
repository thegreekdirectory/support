## The Directions button

Every listing page with a saved address has a **Get Directions** button in the action bar at the top of the page (and in the mobile sticky bar at the bottom). Tapping it launches navigation in your preferred map app.

## Which map app opens?

The app that opens depends on your device and settings:

**In a regular browser (non-PWA):**
- On iPhone/iPad — **Apple Maps** by default
- On Android — **Google Maps** by default
- On desktop — **Google Maps** in a new browser tab

**In the installed PWA app:**
- You can set your preferred map app in the **Settings** tab
- Options are: Apple Maps, Google Maps, Waze, or Ask Every Time
- The default is Ask Every Time — you'll be prompted each time

## What if a listing has no address?

If a listing doesn't have a street address (online-only businesses), no Directions button appears. Some online-only businesses may still show a city and state for general reference.
