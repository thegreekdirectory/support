## Phone

If a phone number is listed, a **Call** button appears in the action bar at the top of the listing page (and in a sticky bar at the bottom on mobile). Tapping it opens your device's phone app with the number pre-dialed.

Phone numbers are stored and displayed in standard US format: (630) 555-1234.

## Email

If an email address is listed, it appears in the contact section. Tapping it opens your device's default email app with the address pre-filled in the To field.

## Website

If a website is listed, a **Website** button appears in the action bar. Tapping it opens the business's website in your browser. In the PWA app, external links open in your system browser instead of inside the app.

## Address

The full address appears in the contact section with a **Get Directions** button next to it. Tapping it launches your preferred map app with the address pre-entered as the destination. See [Getting Directions](/article/users/getting-directions) for more detail.

## What if contact info is missing?

Not all businesses have all contact fields. If a field isn't shown, the business either hasn't added it yet or has chosen not to display it.

If you know a business has a phone number or website that's missing from their listing, use the **Suggest an Edit** button at the bottom of the page to flag it.
