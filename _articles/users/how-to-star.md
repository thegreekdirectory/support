## The star button

Every listing card on the listings page has a **star button** (☆) in the top-right corner of the card's photo. Tap or click it to save that business.

- The star turns **gold** (★) immediately to confirm it's been saved
- A brief animation confirms the action

You can also star from the **individual listing page** — the star button appears in the action bar or alongside the business name at the top.

## No account required

Starring works without a login. Your starred businesses are stored in your browser's cookie storage and (if you've installed the PWA) in your device's local database.

## Starring multiple businesses

You can star as many businesses as you want. There's no limit. All starred businesses appear in your favorites list.

## What happens when you star

When you tap the star:

1. The business ID is added to your `starredListings` cookie
2. If you have the PWA installed, the full listing data and its images are also saved to your device for offline access
3. The star button in every open tab updates immediately via cross-tab sync

See [Viewing Your Starred Listings](/article/users/viewing-starred) to access your favorites.
