## What "Coming Soon" means

A **Coming Soon** badge on a listing means the business is real and Greek-owned, but is not yet fully open. The listing is published early so the community can discover and anticipate it.

Coming Soon listings are fully browsable — you can see the business name, category, location, description, and any contact info the owner has provided. Some fields may be incomplete until the business opens.

## Why listings go up early

Business owners sometimes claim or submit their listing before their doors open, so they can:

- Build awareness in the community ahead of opening
- Collect visibility and social media follows early
- Have their listing ready to go live the moment they open

## How to keep track of a Coming Soon business

**Star it** — Tap the ★ star button on the listing to save it to your favorites. You'll be able to find it easily in your [Starred](/article/users/starring-favorites) list once it opens and updates.

**Check back** — There's no automatic notification system for opening updates currently. Check the listing page again after some time to see if hours and full contact info have been added.

## Finding Coming Soon listings

Use the **Coming Soon** filter on the listings page to browse all businesses with this status. See [Other Filters Explained](/article/users/other-filters).
